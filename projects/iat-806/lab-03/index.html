Lab 03

My 8-bit self, painting and dancing.
Your first left click on the canvas starts the music, "Butterfly Waltz" from StockTune.
Click the canvas to play a sound, flip the big painter's brush, and drop in a new painter in a random color (the background changes too).
Clear screen removes the painters you added. Press space to pause and restart everything, 1, 2 or 3 to freeze just one of the first three painters, and the up/down arrows (or W/S) to change how fast they walk.
