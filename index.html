Hi, I'm Angela Shen.

MA Project based Student at Simon Fraser University, School of Interactive Arts & Technology.

Visual art has always been part of my life. For 10 years, Chinese brush painting helped me recentre, and now I want to share that practice with others.

Through my initiative, "watercolorforall," I run free and low-cost watercolour workshops for young people after high school, while raising awareness of sexual assault resources across Greater Vancouver. Feel free to email me if you'd like to collaborate!
