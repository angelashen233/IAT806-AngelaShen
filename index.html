Hi, I'm Angela Shen.

MA Project based Student at Simon Fraser University, School of Interactive Arts & Technology.

Over the past four years as a communications professional at an Indigenous-led non-profit society, I've focused on trauma-informed, accessible design. That work showed me how much creative practice can support reflection and connection, and I want to share the joy of watercolour as a gentle, reflective method with more people.

As I begin my Master's in Interactive Arts and Technology at SFU, I look forward to learning how to conduct research alongside my social initiative: community watercolour workshops that also raise awareness of sexual assault resources.
